Program Overview

The PS/ES Warrior Production Team gives elementary students hands-on experience with chapel production — cameras, sound, and equipment — working alongside the Middle/High School production crew each week.

Responsibilities

Core expectations for every student on the Production Team:

Before Chapel

Check the schedule and note which chapel days you're assigned to come to school for Production.

Email Mrs. Glavach in advance if you'll be absent so she can find a replacement.

Sign-In & Prayer

Meet Mrs. Suris by Mrs. Brigham's office at 7:25 a.m. to sign in for the day and meet for a time of prayer with Mr. Lopez.

Walking to Chapel

Walk as a group to Chapel — students must be at the front office and walk with the group. If late, go straight to class, NOT to the Lighthouse.

Act in a respectful manner when walking to chapel and handling Production equipment.

Please read, review, and sign the Production Crew Code of Conduct Form and turn it in to Mrs. Brigham on your first assigned day.

Code of Conduct

Maintaining good behavior among students on the PS/ES Warrior Production Team is crucial for a smooth and successful chapel.

Respect & Courtesy

Respect Others — Treat fellow Production members and teachers with kindness and respect at all times.

Listen Carefully — Pay attention when others are speaking or giving instructions and follow directions promptly.

Focus & Engagement

Stay on Task — Focus on your role and responsibilities during chapel.

Be Prepared — Come to chapel prepared to work at your assigned post.

Punctuality & Attendance

Be On Time — Arrive on time. Notify Mrs. Glavach in advance if you will be absent.

Attendance — Attend all scheduled slots unless there's an emergency or a valid reason.

Behavior During Rehearsals

Stay Quiet — Maintain quiet and minimize distractions while others are leading and speaking.

Follow Directions — Obey instructions and feedback. Be open to changes and adjustments.

Safety & Conduct

Follow Safety Rules — Adhere to all safety guidelines, especially when handling equipment.

No Running — Avoid running or playing in the Lighthouse or when walking to chapel.

Positive Attitude

Stay Positive — Maintain a positive attitude and encourage your peers. Avoid complaining or negative behavior.

Support Others — Be supportive of your fellow students, offering help and encouragement when needed.

Conflict Resolution

Handle Disagreements — Address any conflicts calmly and respectfully. Seek help from a teacher if necessary.

Apologize & Forgive — If mistakes are made, apologize and work towards resolving issues. Forgive others and move forward.

Respect for the Lighthouse & Equipment

Keep It Clean — Help keep the Lighthouse clean and tidy. Leave your space better than you found it.

Respect Property — Respect the production equipment. Report any damage immediately.

Post-Performance Behavior

Thank You's — Thank your Production crew and any teachers who helped you.

Reflect Positively — Reflect on your performance positively and be open to feedback for future improvements.

Students who don't follow these expectations may be removed from the Production schedule.
