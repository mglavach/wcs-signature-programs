Program Overview

Our Student Council gives students in grades 3–5 a real voice in school life. Elected representatives attend weekly meetings, share classmates' ideas, and help lead service projects throughout the year.

Who Can Run

Any student in good academic and behavioral standing may apply. Candidates submit an application with their teacher's confirmation, campaign for one week, and are elected by their classmates.

Interest Form - 26–27 School Year Due Thursday, September 10 by 3:30 p.m. - no forms accepted via email or after this date/time.

What Representatives Do

Representatives attend weekly lunch meetings, report back to their class, and help plan and carry out school service projects and activities.

2 Representatives elected per class,
grades 3–5

Officers

Elected representatives vote among themselves for four officer positions, who lead meetings and oversee Student Council responsibilities.

President

Presides over weekly meetings and represents the Council to the school community.

Vice President

Supports the President and steps in to lead meetings when needed.

Treasurer

Helps track funds and materials for Council projects and activities.

Chaplain

Leads devotions and prayer at Council meetings.

Advisors

Faculty advisors guide the program, mentor student leaders, and help ensure every meeting and project runs smoothly.
