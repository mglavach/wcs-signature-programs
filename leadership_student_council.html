What It Is

Through your Student Council, every student's ideas can be heard. It is your responsibility to represent the views of your classmates at each Student Council meeting.

Your school has a Student Council to keep the students, principal, and teachers informed of others' ideas, needs, and opinions. The Student Council also makes sure that everyone in your school cooperates in the day-to-day responsibilities of being part of a school community, enjoys special activities, and works together on specific problems.

  • All students views and ideas are presented to the Student Council through their representatives.
  • Two class representatives are elected by each homeroom to represent their grade level at Student Council meetings.
  • Officers are elected by the class representatives to lead meetings and perform special functions.
  • Advisors are teachers who help guide the Student Council and serve as the link between the Council and the rest of the school staff (see Advisors).
  • The principal, assistant principal and school counselor are members of the Student Council; the final authority with ultimate responsibility for the school.

Officers — elected representatives vote among themselves for three officer positions, who lead meetings and oversee Student Council responsibilities.

  • President — presides over weekly meetings and represents the Council to the school community.
  • Vice President — supports the President and steps in to lead meetings when needed.
  • Treasurer — helps track funds and materials for Council projects and activities.
  • All students nominate and elect Student Council representatives and share their ideas with them.
  • Representatives hold class meetings, listen to classmates' views, and report back on what happens at Student Council meetings. They attend every Student Council meeting.
  • Officers help conduct Student Council meetings, keep records of motions, discussions, and votes, and keep track of Student Council money.
  • Advisors attend all Student Council meetings, teach rules and regulations, offer guidance, and report on Student Council actions to the school staff.
  • The principal attends meetings if needed and is the final authority on all actions of the Student Council.

Student Council members meet on Fridays during lunch. Our first meeting will be on Friday, 10/9.

2 per homeroom - not gender specific.

You will be required to attend scheduled weekly meetings. These meetings will be once a week, during lunch. Members are expected to assist with all service projects and activities throughout the year.

Any student in good academic and behavioral standing may apply. Candidates submit an application, are approved by their grade level teachers, campaign for one week, and are elected by their classmates.

Who is eligible to run for Class Representative? You must...

  • Have earned all A's and B's as a final grade in core subjects on your report card last year. NO EXCEPTIONS.
  • Have earned all 1's and 2's in conduct last year and no disciplinary issues this year. NO EXCEPTIONS.
  • Have no more than one disciplinary referral (sent to principal's office) last year.
  • Be in good standing this year - class and homework assignments turned in regularly, with all A's and B's in core subjects.
