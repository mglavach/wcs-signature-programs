Writing Warriors Academy

At Westminster, we believe every student has a story worth telling and a voice worth developing. The Writing Warriors Academy brings together a range of courses designed for students in second through fifth grade, each one built to strengthen a different piece of the writing journey, from mastering grammar fundamentals to organizing ideas, choosing strong words, and building the confidence to put pen to paper. Whether your child is just starting to write complete sentences or is ready to sharpen more advanced skills, there's a course here to meet them where they are and help them grow into capable, confident writers who love the craft.

Courses

Author Academy

Selected third through fifth graders develop creative writing skills alongside high school mentors, using writer's notebooks to collect and develop ideas. Students publish their very own book and sell it during our annual Entrepreneurship Fair.

Author Academy meets Mondays and Wednesdays, February 1 through March 10.

Days Mondays & Wednesdays
Time 3:00-4:00 p.m.
Dates Feb 1 - Mar 10
Cost TBA

Warrior Lab: Writing Crash Course

Students will learn to correct common grammatical errors, build complete sentences, organize ideas effectively, and select the right words to express their thoughts clearly.

Classes meet Monday through Thursday during a two-week session. Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday classes meet from 3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. Thursday classes meet from 2:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m.

Days Monday-Wednesday (3:00-4:00 p.m.), Thursday (2:00-3:00 p.m.)
Cost $250 per quarter
