Writing Warriors

Have you ever wanted to become a Writing Warrior? The Writing Warriors program brings together two courses for students in second through fifth grade: the Warrior Lab: Writing Crash Course and the Author Academy.

Grades 2nd-5th
Program Time After School
Faculty Mrs. Childers & Mrs. Simril
Writing Warriors

Warrior Lab: Writing Crash Course

Students will learn to correct common grammatical errors, build complete sentences, organize ideas effectively, and select the right words to express their thoughts clearly.

Writing Warriors

Author Academy

Selected third through fifth graders develop creative writing skills alongside high school mentors, using writer's notebooks to collect and develop ideas. Students are published in the Warrior Chronicle literary magazine.

Writing Warriors Timeline

Author Academy runs year-long, while the Crash Course runs as a two-week intensive each quarter.
