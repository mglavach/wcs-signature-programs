Intro to Entrepreneurship: FLINT Prep Series

Intro to Entrepreneurship (FLINT) is a four-week fall course that introduces 4th and 5th grade students to the entrepreneurial mindset and gives every student a real business idea, a first business plan, and a practiced pitch — so they arrive at the spring Entrepreneurship Academy ready to build, not starting from zero. FLINT stays as the fall-term identity; "Intro to Entrepreneurship" is the plain-language name that shows up on the schedule, in the newsletter, or on a report card line where parents need to instantly know what it is.

How the Four Lessons Connect

Each lesson hands the next one something concrete to build on — nothing is a one-off:

  • Application — surfaces who a student is (interests, skills, mindset) and why they want in.
  • Find Your Problem, Find Your Business — turns that self-knowledge into a real business idea, rooted in a problem the student has personally observed.
  • Build Your Business Plan — turns the idea into a structured plan (customer, product, competition) using the vocabulary the spring Academy will assume they already know.
  • Perfect Your Pitch — turns the plan into a rehearsed, spoken pitch — the exact skill they'll need for the Academy pitch and the Business Fair.

Entrepreneurship Academy 101

WCS's youngest entrepreneurs from 4th and 5th grade will learn how to start and run a business while having fun. Entrepreneurship Academy 101 is an 11-week program where students are introduced to basic concepts of production/manufacturing, marketing, financing, and organizational development while implementing a biblical perspective on their own business.

During These Sessions, Our Entrepreneurs Will Learn To:

  • Choose a product or service and develop a business plan.
  • Set goals and how to stay on track.
  • Raise capital to finance his/her business and repay the loans.
  • Calculate a basic Profit and Loss Statement (P&L).
  • Calculate how much to pay in tithes.
  • Showcase their business and give a "pitch" (presentation) about his/her model.
  • Promote their business at the 4th Annual Warrior Business Fair.
Days Fridays
Cost $225 per child

Entrepreneurship Academy 201

This session is for all 5th grade students who previously participated in Entrepreneurship Academy 101. Now that you know the basics, it's time to take your business adventure to the next level.

During These Sessions We Will Go In-Depth In The Following Topics:

  • How to write a business plan.
  • Investing opportunities.
  • Expanding your business.
  • Marketing strategies.
  • Ways to raise capital.
  • Pitching, showcasing, and promoting your business at the 4th Annual Warrior Business Fair.
Days Thursdays
Cost $225 per child
