    Warrior Wrap-UpRules of Order

    Warrior Rules of Order

    How we run every meeting, so every voice is heard.

    1. One Voice at a Time

      • Only one person talks at a time. No talking over classmates.

    2. Raise It Before You Say It

      • Want to share an idea? Raise your hand and wait to be called on by the President.

    3. Stay on Topic

      • Keep your comments about what's being discussed, not something else.

    4. Disagree Kindly

      • It's okay to have a different idea. Say it respectfully, no putting anyone down.

    5. The President Leads

      • The President runs the meeting and decides who speaks next. Respect their lead.

    6. Ideas Become Motions

      • If you want the council to decide on something, say: "I move that we..." Then the group votes.

    7. Majority Rules

      • When we vote, most votes wins. If your idea doesn't win, that's okay, we're still a team.

    8. Listen to Understand

      • When someone else is talking, listen instead of planning what you'll say next.

    Weekly Meeting Agenda

    The order we follow every Friday. Each wrap-up page summarizes these sections.

    Date: Place: Pavilion by the SAC Recorded by:

    1. Call to Order & Prayer

      2 min
      • President welcomes everyone and calls the meeting to order.
      • Chaplain opens with a short prayer.

    2. Roll Call

      2 min
      • Take attendance — make sure every class is represented.
      • Note any absences for the record.

    3. Welcome & Last Week's Recap

      3 min
      • Quick reminder of what we talked about last time.
      • Any corrections or updates?

    4. Officer Updates

      5 min
      • President: What's happening with Student Leadership this week?
      • Vice President: Any upcoming events to know about?
      • Chaplain: Spiritual focus, chapel planning, or a verse of the week.

    5. Committee Updates

      5 min
      • Nature Ambassadors: progress, ideas, or upcoming activities.
      • Sunshine Squad: ways we're spreading positivity this week.
      • Community Connect: outreach opportunities and plans.

    6. Old Business

      3 min
      • Check in on projects or ideas from previous meetings.
      • Is there anything still left to finish?

    7. New Business & Ideas

      5 min
      • Share new ideas from your class.
      • Discuss any new projects, events, or service opportunities.
      • Decide who will help with what, and by when.

    8. Announcements & Reminders

      2 min
      • Important dates or deadlines coming up.
      • Reminders for the whole school, if any.

    9. Closing

      1 min
      • Thank everyone for coming and for representing their class well.
      • Confirm the date, time, and place of next week's meeting.
