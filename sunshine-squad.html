Program Overview

Sunshine Squad gives 4th grade students at the Elementary Campus the chance to serve before school, brightening the school community through encouragement, kindness, and small acts of service.

Responsibilities

Sunshine Squad members report at 7:30 a.m. on their scheduled duty days and sign in with Mrs. Glavach before the school day begins. As a member of the Sunshine Squad, I promise to:

Attitude & Character

Be Kind – Treat every student and adult with respect, patience, and kindness.

Be Positive – Greet others with a smile and share joy each morning.

Be a Role Model – Show younger students how Warriors should act in words and actions.

Responsibility & Commitment

Be On Time – Arrive by 7:15 AM sharp on my assigned day.

Be Ready for Class – Leave PK at the first bell and be in my homeroom by 7:55 AM.

Be Dependable – If I can't make my day, I will let Mrs. Glavach know ahead of time.

Leadership in Action

Be Engaged – Play with and include all students, not just my friends.

Be Responsible – Help set up and clean up games and toys.

Be Flexible – Listen to Morning Care teachers and follow directions quickly.

Be a Peacemaker – Help solve small problems kindly, and ask an adult for help if needed.
