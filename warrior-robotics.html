Warrior Robotics

Warrior Robotics gives students hands-on experience in engineering, coding, and problem-solving through competitive VEX IQ Robotics teams. Working collaboratively, students design, build, program, and test robots, developing innovation, teamwork, perseverance, and strategic thinking through the engineering design process and robotics competitions.
