Thanksgiving Food Drive

A Student Council Community Connect event

At a Glance

This year, WCS Student Leadership is partnering with Caring for Miami (Elementary School) and Be the Light (Middle and High School) for a Community Connect Thanksgiving Food Drive to serve those in need across South Florida.

In Matthew 25:34–40, Jesus teaches that when we care for those who are hungry and in need, we are serving Him. Join us November 2–20 as we put our faith into action, becoming the hands and feet of Jesus and sharing His love through generosity and service.

Head, Heart & Hands Connection

The Thanksgiving Food Drive is part of the Community Connect Pathway, which grows students through Head, Heart, and Hands.

Head · Think

Purpose • Learning • Awareness

Students learn about hunger and need right here in South Florida, and how Caring for Miami and Be the Light serve our neighbors. Through Matthew 25:34–40, they discover that caring for those in need is serving Jesus, and they see real-world impact: $25 feeds a family for a week.

Heart · Be

Character • Values • Mindset

This quarter, students are growing from gratitude for what they have to compassion for others. On November 3, following chapel, students will walk through the Caring for Miami bus to learn how this ministry serves our neighbors. Praying Dads will pray over the students and offer a blessing for the Caring for Miami ministry team. A Noisy Offering container beside the bus will give students an opportunity to give with joy. Through learning, prayer, and generosity, students will discover how service can become part of who they are.

Hands · Do

Service • Leadership • Action

Students put their faith into action by stacking the shelf with their grade-level items, giving to the Noisy Offering, and joining Student Leadership in loading and delivering bags to our community partners as the hands and feet of Jesus.
