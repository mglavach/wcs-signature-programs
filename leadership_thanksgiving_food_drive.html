At a Glance

This year, WCS Student Leadership is partnering with Caring for Miami (Elementary School) and Be the Light (Middle and High School) for a Community Connect Thanksgiving Food Drive to serve those in need across South Florida.

In Matthew 25:34–40, Jesus teaches that when we care for those who are hungry and in need, we are serving Him. Join us November 2–20 as we put our faith into action, becoming the hands and feet of Jesus and sharing His love through generosity and service.
