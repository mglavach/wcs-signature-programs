WCS Signature Programs: Elementary Pathway

Why a Signature Program Pathway on the Elementary Level?

  1. Develop God-Given Gifts — Help students recognize that their talents, abilities, and interests are part of God's unique design and purpose for their lives.
  2. Discover Strengths and Passions Early — Provide opportunities for students to explore a variety of interests and identify areas where they excel and find joy.
  3. Build Essential Life Skills — Develop critical thinking, communication, collaboration, creativity, leadership, and problem-solving skills that benefit students in every area of learning.
  4. Encourage Exploration and Innovation — Expose students to experiences in STEM, entrepreneurship, mass media and service before selecting a specialized pathway in high school.
  5. Create a Connected K–12 Journey — Establish a strong foundation that prepares students for future Signature Program opportunities while fostering a culture of excellence, curiosity, and lifelong learning.

Current Programs

Biomedical Science

Biomedical Mentorship Program

Through hands-on lessons and mentorship from Westminster's high school Biomedical Science students, elementary students explore topics such as nutrition, wellness, and healthy lifestyle choices. Students develop scientific curiosity, critical thinking skills, and an appreciation for how biomedical professionals serve and care for others while gaining early exposure to the field of health sciences.

HOSA Jr.

HOSA Jr. introduces fourth-grade students to the exciting world of biomedical science through interactive lessons, hands-on activities, and real-world medical connections. Guided by high school HOSA mentors, students explore the human body, develop critical thinking and teamwork skills, and gain exposure to a variety of healthcare and medical career fields. This enrichment opportunity fosters curiosity, scientific inquiry, and a deeper understanding of how science can be used to serve others.

Nutrition 101

Elementary students learn how colorful foods help their bodies grow strong and stay healthy through engaging lessons led by WCS high school nutrition students. Participants explore nutrition, wellness, and healthy habits while taking part in fun challenges that encourage them to make smart choices and try new foods. Through this experience, students discover how healthy living supports both their bodies and their ability to serve God and others.
