Why a Signature Program Pathway on the Elementary Level?

  1. Develop God-Given Gifts — Help students recognize that their talents, abilities, and interests are part of God's unique design and purpose for their lives.
  2. Discover Strengths and Passions Early — Provide opportunities for students to explore a variety of interests and identify areas where they excel and find joy.
  3. Build Essential Life Skills — Develop critical thinking, communication, collaboration, creativity, leadership, and problem-solving skills that benefit students in every area of learning.
  4. Encourage Exploration and Innovation — Expose students to experiences in STEM, entrepreneurship, mass media and service before selecting a specialized pathway in high school.
  5. Create a Connected K–12 Journey — Establish a strong foundation that prepares students for future Signature Program opportunities while fostering a culture of excellence, curiosity, and lifelong learning.

Current Programs

Entrepreneurship

Warrior Entrepreneurship Academy 101

4th and 5th graders learn to start and run a business through a biblical lens, covering production, marketing, financing, and organizational development. Students cap the program by pitching at the annual Warrior Business Fair.

Warrior Entrepreneurship Academy 2.0

Built for 5th grade EA 101 alumni, this advanced session goes deeper into business planning, investing, expansion strategies, and marketing, culminating in another showcase at the Warrior Business Fair.

Nature Ambassador

Students explore God's creation, develop an appreciation for the natural world, and inspire others to care for the environment through stewardship and service. Through learning and leadership opportunities, students become advocates for protecting and preserving the world God has entrusted to us.

Safety Patrol

Selected 5th grade students serve the Elementary Campus during morning drop-off, helping create a safe and welcoming environment for students and families. Through this leadership opportunity, students develop responsibility, dependability, and servant leadership skills.

Student Ambassador Program

Selected 4th and 5th grade students represent the Elementary Campus at special events, welcome prospective families, and serve as positive role models while developing communication, confidence, responsibility, and servant leadership skills.

Student Council

Selected 3rd–5th grade students serve the Elementary Campus through elected leadership positions, representing their peers and helping plan initiatives that strengthen the school community. Campaigning begins each September, providing students with opportunities to develop leadership, communication, collaboration, and civic responsibility while serving others.

STEM

Mathletes

Students strengthen mathematical thinking through challenging problems, collaborative activities, and real-world applications. This competitive program develops logical reasoning, problem-solving, perseverance, and analytical thinking through team-based competitions — building confidence and celebrating academic excellence while preparing students for future opportunities in STEM, engineering, research, and business.

SessionYear-long Program TimeAfter School Grades3rd–5th (selected) LiaisonMrs. Simril

Warrior Robotics

Students gain hands-on experience in engineering, coding, and problem-solving through competitive VEX IQ Robotics teams. Working collaboratively to design, build, program, and test robots, students develop innovation, teamwork, perseverance, and strategic thinking through the engineering design process and robotics competitions.
